Hardware

Designing the physical interfaces and electronics that connect digital logic to the real world. From schematic capture to rapid prototyping and fabrication.

PCB Design & EDA

Schematic capture, footprint design, and trace routing using KiCad. Focused on power management circuits, sensor integration, and optimizing layouts for compact wearable forms.

3D Modeling & CAD

Creating functional mechanical parts and enclosures using Autodesk Fusion 360. Validating spatial constraints and generating high-fidelity environment simulations with Twinmotion.

Rapid Prototyping

Iterative hardware fabrication utilizing FDM 3D Printing. Experienced in material optimization (PLA, PETG) on platforms like Bambu Lab for achieving precise tolerances in functional assemblies.

Power Systems & Distribution

Designing mains-powered distribution for multi-subsystem machines. Separating motor and logic rails to keep switching noise off sensing electronics, and using buck regulation to make drive voltage a tunable design parameter rather than a fixed constant.

Software

Building the logic pipelines, machine learning models, and embedded firmware that bring physical devices and raw data to life.

Core Languages

Developing robust data pipelines with Python, programming low-latency embedded systems with C++, and conducting statistical evaluations with R.

Event-Based Vision & Real-Time Tracking

Building embedded computer-vision pipelines on event-based sensors (Prophesee GENX320 / OpenMV), where pixels report brightness change asynchronously rather than on a fixed frame clock. Blob detection, multi-object trajectory tracking with motion prediction, and coincidence-loss modelling for high-rate counting.

Machine Learning

Fine-tuning Large Language Models (LLMs) leveraging QLoRA and Unsloth for memory-efficient parameter adaptation, mapping outputs via PCA analysis.

Web Systems & UI

Designing responsive, data-driven interfaces using HTML5, CSS3, and Tailwind CSS. Bridging the gap between IoT backends and user-facing dashboards.

Version Control (Git)
