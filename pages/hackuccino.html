Hackuccino: The Moving Duck Alarm

Mechanical Design x IoT Connectivity

/ Project Overview

Developed a "moving duck" alarm clock designed to prevent oversleeping by forcing physical interaction. Built within a 48-hour hackathon sprint under the "Sleep" theme, the project combines mechanical design with IoT connectivity to create a persistent wake-up solution.

/ Mechanical Gait Demo

A demonstration of the mechanical walking gait developed for our Hackathon 2025 project. The video showcases the synchronized servo-motor control used to create a persistent "chase-to-snooze" alarm, integrated with a Telegram Bot API.
